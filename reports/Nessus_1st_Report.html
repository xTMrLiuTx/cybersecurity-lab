Overall Risk Rating: Critical

The target environment is highly exposed. All core services are running in their default, un-hardened configurations, creating multiple low-effort entry points for an attacker. Top 3 Immediate Remediation Actions
  1. Patching — Update All Services Apache, MySQL, Nginx, OpenSSH, and Samba must be updated to their latest security-patched releases. Several running versions carry public CVEs with working exploits.
  2. Authentication — Enforce MFA and Eliminate Default Credentials All services accept generic or default passwords. Multi-Factor Authentication must be enforced and a strong, unique password policy applied to every service account.
  3. Network Hardening — Minimal Port Exposure and Firewall Policy All tested ports are openly accessible with no visible egress filtering. Implement a deny-all firewall policy, restrict access to required ports only, and apply VLAN segmentation between services.
